# Homepage article artwork

The six 960 × 600 WebP covers in `public/one-aminos/article-covers/` combine One Aminos product photography with monochrome backgrounds. The source photos are from the One Aminos library’s `site/product-images-labeled/` directory.

| Cover | Photography | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New to Peptides? | BPC-157 vial | White plinth |
| Reconstitution Guide | GHK-Cu and bacteriostatic water vials | Lab tray |
| Syringe & Measurement Guide | Bacteriostatic water vial | Graduated scale |
| Peptide Storage Guide | Semax, KPV and BPC-157 vials | Lab shelf |
| What Is GHK-Cu? | GHK-Cu vial | Round platform |
| What Is Retatrutide? | Three GLP-3RT vials | Tiered platforms |

The demo uses label crops from the GHK-Cu and bacteriostatic water product photos.
