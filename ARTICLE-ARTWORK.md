## Guide illustrations

ImageGen used the original guide illustrations and One Aminos product photography as references. The 1040 × 693 WebP files are used in the beginner and reconstitution guides.

### Powder and solution

`public/one-aminos/guide-art/powder-and-solution.webp`

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the first photograph for the One Aminos beginner research guide in calm black, white and neutral silver. Keep the two identical closed glass vials at the same scale and baseline, with clear glass below each label visibly revealing a white powder cake on the left and clear colorless liquid on the right. Match the label typography from reference 2: large black ONE with smaller tracked AMINOS directly underneath on plain matte white label. Make the label compact enough to show both materials below. Neither vial has a compound name, strength, or numeric dose. White plastic caps, metallic silver collars. Replace all blue and other colors with a neutral light gray, uncluttered seamless lab bench background. Soft daylight and realistic contact shadows. Keep the pair centered with good wide margins. No wording except ONE and AMINOS on the labels, no added watermarks, no in-image caption, no human or operation. Landscape 3:2.

### Lab supplies

`public/one-aminos/guide-art/lab-supplies.webp`

Use case: compositing. Edit the first photograph to make a black, white, and neutral gray editorial website illustration for a laboratory research supplies list. Brand the two closed vials prominently using the black ONE above tracked AMINOS art from reference vial packshots 2 and 3. White labels and white or silver lids. The left vial can say RESEARCH SAMPLE, the right one BAC WATER. Keep the supplies clearly staged, with a wrapped capped syringe, sealed swab packets, blank white labels, a black pen, and a closed opaque gray sharps container in the background. Remove every former background logo and colored accent, plus the plant and decorative background molecules. Use a clean neutral light gray lab counter and a seamless off-white wall with soft daylight, gentle contact shadows and no blue tint, no orange or red. No human, no action, no exposed needle, no measurement value. Landscape 3:2 with modest margins and the branded vials easy to see.
